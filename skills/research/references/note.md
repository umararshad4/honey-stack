# Research note format

Use only sections that the question needs. Keep the decision and its source trail together.

```markdown
# <Question>
Checked: YYYY-MM-DD. Scope: <project, versions, market or jurisdiction>.

## Recommendation
<Answer and why it satisfies the hard constraints.>

## Constraints
<Requirements and exclusions supplied by the user or verified from the repo.>

## Evidence
| Claim | Finding | Source / version / date | Confidence or limitation |
|---|---|---|---|

## Alternatives
<Tradeoffs and concrete reasons rejected options do not fit.>

## Unknowns
<Conflicts, inaccessible evidence, single-source claims, and what would settle them.>

## Handoff
<Implementation constraints, facts to refresh, and choices still owned by the user.>
```

Confidence reflects the evidence, not the number of pages found. Do not assign numeric scores unless a scoring rubric actually exists.
